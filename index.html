Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our 80% discount wholesale price list, legal green cracker standards, parcel logistics, and WhatsApp enquiry ordering.

How can I order crackers from Mathura Crackers in Sivakasi? expand_more

As per the 2018 Supreme Court of India directive, direct automated online payment checkouts for fireworks are strictly prohibited. To order, browse our online catalog, select your desired items and packaging quantities to build your custom enquiry list, and submit it directly to our team via WhatsApp (+91 70103 17389 / +91 74180 51084 / +91 72009 36916). Our godown personnel verify live inventory, confirm transport parcel service to your town, and share official bank/UPI details for secure processing.

What discount is offered on the Mathura Crackers price list? expand_more

Mathura Crackers offers a flat 80% discount off standard printed MRP on all 137+ fireworks items in our catalog. This includes flower pots, ground chakkars, sparklers, aerial repeater cakes, sound crackers, and gift boxes. Each product card transparently displays the original printed MRP, the 80% discount rate, and the exact wholesale selling price.

Where is the Mathura Crackers store and godown located? expand_more

Our store and dispatch warehouse is located at 4/190, Mettamalai, Sattur - 626203, Virudhunagar District, Tamil Nadu. We are open for direct customer visits, wedding pyrotechnic consultations, and order pickups daily from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST.

Are Mathura Crackers products genuine, certified green fireworks? expand_more

Yes. 100% of our products are certified green crackers formulated in strict compliance with CSIR-NEERI guidelines and PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation) standards. Our formulations eliminate hazardous chemicals such as barium nitrate, lithium, and arsenic, resulting in 30–35% particulate matter reduction while ensuring vibrant illumination and statutory decibel limits.

What are the minimum order values and delivery timelines? expand_more

The minimum order enquiry value is ₹3,000 for Tamil Nadu and ₹5,000 for other Indian states. Consignments are packed in heavy-duty corrugated cartons as per the Indian Explosives Act and routed through registered parcel transport carriers to your nearest district logistics hub with consignment LR copies sent directly to your WhatsApp.
